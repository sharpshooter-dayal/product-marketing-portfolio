Digital Divide Data

3x Search Visibility for Digital Divide Data

Problem

DDD’s website was averaging 240K monthly impressions at position 20.8, buried on page 2 for most queries. The site used internal technical language instead of buyer language and had no content pillar structure. Organic search was not converting.

Solution
  • Wrote content for 100+ pages across the entire DDD website, rewriting every page in benefits-first, SEO-structured language and launching on schedule in February 2026
  • Built a blog content engine of 618 ranking URLs publishing 5 blogs per week, with keyword gap analysis against Scale AI, Appen, iMerit, and Sama identifying 5 untapped keyword territories
  • Mapped every high-priority query to a specific target page, ensuring each page had a structured, search-intent answer optimized for both Google and AI engines
Impact
  • Monthly impressions grew from 240K to 700K within weeks of launch, average Google position improved from 20.8 to 7.5
  • 55.6% of total site search visibility now comes from blog content, with 383 blogs in Google’s top 10
  • Organic became DDD’s number 1 converting channel with 625 key events, outperforming direct traffic on fewer sessions
Digital Divide Data

34% Lead-to-Call Conversion Through HubSpot Cadences

Lifecycle & Demand Generation

Problem

DDD had no structured nurture system to move inbound leads toward a sales conversation. Follow-up was manual, inconsistent, and slow, averaging 6 days from lead capture to first sales touch. Sales had no reliable pipeline of warm, pre-educated leads to work from.

Solution
  • Built 16+ HubSpot cadences across 8 market segments including AV and ADAS, Humanoid and Physical AI, Robotics, Retail and Geospatial, and Oracle ABM with 4+ division-specific variants in 30-day/12-touch and 45-day/15-touch versions
  • Segmented leads by vertical and behavior so each contact received messaging specific to their industry pain point, with persona-routing rules ensuring no account received two sequences simultaneously
  • Every email claim traced to a live DDD page with research-backed subject lines and human tone throughout, keeping deliverability clean and messaging defensible
Impact
  • 34% of nurtured leads booked a sales call within 30 days, time-to-first-touch cut from 6 days to under 24 hours
  • 32% average open rate and 5% CTR across all cadences, with 21% of opens converting to MQLs
  • Reduced manual sales follow-up by an estimated 8 hours per week across the team
